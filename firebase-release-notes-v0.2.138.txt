Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.138 (build 141)

NEW: "Prefer offroad"

Surface now has three settings:

  Paved only      never routes onto unpaved
  Allow offroad   unpaved is permitted, not sought
  Prefer offroad  picks the loop with the most unpaved

Be aware of what the third one can and cannot do. The router can be told to
AVOID unpaved; there is no opposite instruction, so we cannot ask it to go
looking for gravel. What "Prefer offroad" actually does is generate the loops
without the restriction, measure how much of each one is unpaved, and show you
the one with the most. If the area has no gravel, it will honestly return
tarmac rather than inventing something.

So that you can check it rather than take our word for it, every result now
shows its unpaved share — "23% unpaved" next to the climb and time. That
number is measured from the actual roads in the route, not estimated. Loops
with no unpaved at all simply omit it.

One deliberate limit: safety still comes first. If the grittiest loop spends a
long way on genuinely dangerous roads and a tarmac one does not, you get the
tarmac one. A recreational preference does not get to override the thing the
app exists for. In practice they rarely fight — unpaved tracks are usually
car-free, so they already score well.

Also note this means unpaved, not difficult. We have no data on how technical
a track is, so a smooth gravel path and a rooty singletrack look identical to
us. Nothing in the app claims otherwise.

What to try
- Set Prefer offroad and compare the percentages against Allow offroad on the
  same distance and direction. Does the number actually go up?
- Look at a high-percentage loop on the map. Does it match what you know is
  gravel around there?
- Somewhere with no gravel at all, confirm it degrades quietly to 0% rather
  than doing something odd.

Unchanged from build 140
- "Start ride" confirms in place; it does not launch navigation yet.
- You cannot save a loop.
- Off-route on a loop shows the off-course banner and will not reroute you.

Only works in the 31 countries our safety road data covers.
