Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.137 (build 140)

Three changes to the loop finder, all from your build 139 feedback.

1. Start ride is now the persistent button
   Once loops are on screen, the always-visible button at the bottom is
   "Start ride" and it acts on whichever loop you have selected. Before you
   have searched, it is still "Find loops".

2. Try another moved under the loops
   It is a secondary button at the end of the list it would replace, so
   searching again is a deliberate act rather than the thing your thumb lands
   on. The remaining-sessions counter moved with it — it now sits next to the
   button that actually spends it, wherever that button is.

3. Five loops per search instead of eight
   Every candidate is a network round trip you wait through, and once the
   direction arc narrowed them the last few bearings were producing near
   copies of their neighbours — more waiting, no more choice. Five still
   samples both edges of the arc and its middle. The wait should be roughly a
   third shorter.

What to check
- After a search, is "Start ride" where your thumb expects it?
- Does "Try another" still feel findable at the bottom of the list?
- Does the shorter search feel better, or do five loops feel too few? This is
  a judgement call and I would rather have your read on it than guess.
- The counter should say the same thing in both places and never disagree
  with itself.

Unchanged from build 139
- "Start ride" confirms in place; it does not hand off to navigation yet, so
  the button is testable but does not launch a ride.
- You cannot save a loop.
- Off-route on a loop shows the off-course banner and will not reroute you,
  deliberately: on a loop your destination is where you started, so a normal
  reroute would cut the ride short.

Only works in the 31 countries our safety road data covers.
