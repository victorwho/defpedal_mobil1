Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.151 (build 154)

FIX: loops should stop arriving as "a loop plus detours".

You said loops had out-and-back sections hanging off them — ride up a road, turn
round, come back. You were right, and my last build caused it.

Why it happened

The app measured "how much of this loop is road you ride twice" as one number.
Two completely different things landed in that number:

- A DETOUR: up a road, turn round, back down it. This is the thing that makes a
  loop stop feeling like one ride.
- THE ROAD OUT OF TOWN: you leave on it and come home on it. Also road ridden
  twice — but unavoidable in a valley, and it still feels like a loop.

Last build I raised the limit on that single number, because at its old setting
nothing could pass. That was the right call for the road-out-of-town case and
the wrong one for detours: raising it let exactly the loops you're describing
through.

Measured at 30 km: Bucharest loops repeat 15-30% of themselves, but almost none
of it is detours — it's all the shared way out. Around Rasnov the same range
hides single detours of 6.0 km, 6.8 km and 4.0 km.

What changed

The app can now tell them apart. A detour ends in a U-turn, so the same stretch
of road appears twice back-to-back. The road out of town is ridden twice at
opposite ends of the ride and never back-to-back. That difference is exact.

- Loops with more than 8% detour are rejected.
- When ranking loops, detours now matter MORE than the total repeated road — so
  a loop that shares the way out but never doubles back beats one that repeats
  less overall but has a detour in it.
- Where a result does contain one, the note now says it in kilometres:
  "Includes 1.4 km of out-and-back detour". Turns at junctions and one-way pairs
  under 200 m are ignored — that's just how roads work.

What to check

- Do loops read as ONE ride now? That's the whole point of this build.
- Bucharest-style grid: should be clean.
- Around Rasnov: EVERY loop I measured there has a detour above the limit, so
  the app will still offer you the least bad one and label it. Please check
  whether the least-detoured one now feels acceptable. If it doesn't, the answer
  is probably a longer minimum distance rather than a stricter limit — the
  terrain may simply not hold a detour-free 30 km loop.
- If you see a loop labelled with a detour, look at it on the map. Does the
  label match what you see? The detection is new and I want to know if it's
  calling things wrong.

Unchanged
- Five results per search, all measured.
- Saved loops sync to your account.
- Scenic scoring still covers the Rasnov area only.
