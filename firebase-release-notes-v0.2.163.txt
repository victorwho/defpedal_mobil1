Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.163 (build 166)

Fixes the elevation chart trapping itself open. One change, nothing else.

THE BUG

You opened the elevation chart from the side button, and the chart then covered
that button — so there was no way to close it again mid-ride.

THE FIX: the chart now has its own X

Top-right corner of the chart card, next to the current-elevation number. Tap
it and the chart closes. The glyph is small but the tap area is padded out to
roughly 44pt, which is what a gloved hand on a moving bike needs.

WHY IT IS AN X AND NOT "I MOVED THINGS APART"

I could have shuffled the chart and the button until they stopped colliding.
That is screen-height arithmetic, and it is exactly the reasoning that put the
side buttons on top of the instruction panel one build ago — a number that was
right on my assumptions and wrong on your phone. Whether those two elements
collide depends on your screen height, your system bars, and whether the
"Steep" pill happens to be showing. A card that carries its own way out cannot
be trapped by any of that.

WHAT TO CHECK

- Open the elevation chart, then close it with the X. Then open and close it a
  few times. It should work every time regardless of where you are on a route.
- The side elevation button still toggles the chart too, when it is not covered.
  Both routes work; the X is the one that always works.
- The X sits next to the elevation reading in the chart's top-right. Tell me if
  it is hard to hit while moving — I can make the target bigger.

KNOWN, AND YOUR CALL

While the chart is open it still sits ON TOP of the elevation button, so that
button is hidden until you close the chart. The X means this no longer traps
you, so I left it there rather than adding a second change I could not test
from here. If you would rather the buttons floated above the chart so the
elevation button stays visible, say so — it is a one-line change, but it would
put the buttons over the right-hand edge of the chart instead.
