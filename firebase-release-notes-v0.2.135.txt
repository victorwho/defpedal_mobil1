Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.135 (build 138)

NEW: Find a loop

A ride that brings you back where you started. No destination to type — you
pick how far, what terrain, whether offroad is allowed, and which way to head
out, and the app finds loops that finish at your feet.

Where to find it: route planning screen → the circular-arrow button in the
right-hand column, under the Nearby (layers) button.

What to try
- Pick a distance, tap "Find loops", and watch the map. Each loop draws as it
  is found, so you can see it searching rather than staring at a spinner.
- Drag the ring on the map to steer which way the loop heads out. There is
  also a "Which way" row in the sheet that does the same thing from a list.
- Try a terrain the area cannot deliver (ask for Hilly in a flat city). It
  should tell you honestly what it checked, not silently give you something
  else. Every result that had to bend one of your choices says so.
- Try "Try another" a few times. Loops accumulate rather than replacing each
  other, so nothing you liked disappears.
- Tap Cancel mid-search. Nothing should be charged and nothing should keep
  running in the background.

What I most want to know
1. Do the loops feel like rides you would actually do, or do they wander
   somewhere pointless?
2. Is the wait (roughly 3–8 seconds) tolerable, and does watching the loops
   appear make it feel shorter?
3. Does dragging the direction ring fight with panning the map? This is the
   one interaction that cannot be tested off a real phone, so it is the most
   likely thing to be wrong.
4. Is the distance you asked for close enough to the distance you got?

Known gaps in this build
- "Start ride" on a loop confirms in place; it does not yet hand off to the
  route preview screen or start navigation. Reviewing the loop is the thing
  to test, not riding it.
- You cannot save a loop yet.
- Going off-route on a loop shows the off-course banner. It will not reroute
  you — that is deliberate, because on a loop your destination is where you
  started, so an ordinary reroute would end the ride early. Guiding you back
  onto the line is a later build.

Only works in the 31 countries our safety road data covers. Outside them the
button is visible but dimmed and explains why.
