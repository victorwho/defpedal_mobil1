Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.148 (build 151)

FIX: five loops per search, not ten — and all five measured.

You were getting ten. Three places disagreed about how many to show, and the
one whose comment said it was in charge wasn't being used: the constant read
"Loops shown at once = 3", but the list had its own hardcoded cap of 12, and
results streaming in bypassed the constant entirely. The search runs two rounds
that each produce five candidates, so ten arrived and ten were shown.

The worse half of that bug: only three of the ten were ever measured. The other
seven showed a dash where the climb figure should be. Ten rows you can't
compare isn't ten choices.

Now: five loops, each with real climb and risk.

What did NOT change, on purpose

The search still generates ten candidates behind the scenes and offers you the
best five. Finding a loop that doesn't double back on itself depends heavily on
which direction and shape it tries — around Rasnov, whether ANY candidate
qualifies comes down to that. Generating five instead of ten would give you a
shorter list of worse loops, not the same loops sooner.

So this is a clutter fix, not a speed fix. Search takes about as long as before.
If the WAIT was the problem rather than the list length, say so and I'll halve
the generation too — that's a real trade (fewer, worse loops, roughly half the
wait) and it should be your call, not mine.
