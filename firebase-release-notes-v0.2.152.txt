Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.152 (build 155)

FIX: the paved/unpaved numbers were wrong on every route, always.

You reported loops with obvious offroad sections claiming to be 100% paved. They
were — and so was every other route the app has ever measured.

What was wrong

The router reports which roads are unpaved. The app was looking for that
information in the wrong place in the response — a field that does not exist and
never has. So it found nothing, every time, and "nothing unpaved found" came out
as "100% paved".

The data was there the whole time. On a Rasnov route, 20 of 65 junctions are
flagged unpaved.

What the same routes report now

  Rasnov -> Poiana Brasov    was 100% paved   ->  55% unpaved (7.5 km)
  Rasnov -> Zarnesti         was 100% paved   ->  54% unpaved (12.2 km)
  Rasnov -> Bran             was 100% paved   ->  45% unpaved (7.4 km)

With "avoid unpaved" on, those same routes come back at 0-2% unpaved.

Two more things this was breaking

TUNNEL AND BRIDGE MARKERS HAVE NEVER WORKED. They read the same wrong field. The
map markers, the warnings as you approach, and the icon set that ships with them
have had nothing to draw since they were added. Unprotected left turns were the
only part of that feature that ever worked. Now fixed too — if you ride through a
tunnel or over a bridge, you should see it.

The route composition breakdown was permanently empty for the same reason.

And: "avoid unpaved" could be silently ignored

The loop screen read your saved preference once, at the moment it opened. If the
app hadn't finished loading your settings by then, it kept the default and quietly
ignored it — the pill would show "Allow offroad" even though your saved setting
said otherwise. It now follows your setting whenever it loads, and still lets you
override it per search.

What to check

- Generate loops around Rasnov and look at the surface bar. Do the percentages
  match what the map shows? THIS is the thing to check — the numbers are new and I
  want to know if they read wrong.
- Turn on "avoid unpaved" in Profile, then open the loop planner. The surface pill
  should already be on "Paved only" without you touching it.
- Ride or preview a route with a tunnel or a bridge. A marker should appear. It
  never has before, so this is effectively a new feature.
- If a loop still puts you on trails with "Paved only" selected, tell me — there's
  one open question about which trail types the router counts as unpaved, and your
  answer decides whether it's worth chasing.

Not fixed, and honestly stated

The router marks a way unpaved if it has an unpaved surface tag, or if it's a
"track". A forest path mapped without any surface information may not be counted —
so it would be neither excluded nor shown in the percentage. I could not verify
this against the live routing profile from here. If you see trails on a "Paved
only" route, that's the likely cause and it's a routing-server change, not an app
one.

Unchanged
- Five results per search, detour detection, saved loops syncing to your account.
- Scenic scoring still covers the Rasnov area only.
