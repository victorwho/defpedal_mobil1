Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.150 (build 153)

FIX: loops should come back the right length now — and more of them should
ride out of town before looping.

You said the ride-out-then-loop shape still wasn't showing up often enough. I
measured the mechanism against the live router instead of guessing, and found
three faults. Only one was about that shape.

1. The size model was wrong by a factor of two

The generator guesses how big a ring needs to be, routes it, then corrects. The
guess assumed real roads are 1.25x longer than the straight-line shape. I routed
120 rings across five cities: the real figure is 2.11x for a 3-point ring and
2.54x for a 6-point one.

So every first attempt came back about 86% too long, one correction only got to
~26% off, and the limit for "close enough" is 12%. With two attempts allowed,
almost nothing ever landed on the length you asked for. At Bucharest 30 km, ZERO
of ten rings passed. The search then quietly relaxed its own length filter on
basically every run — which is why loops came back the wrong distance, and why
so few survived to be offered at all.

Fixed and re-measured: loops hitting the requested length went from 0-1 in 5 to
4-5 in 5.

2. Every ride-out loop was built as a hexagon

The code picked the ring shape (3 or 6 points) and the ring-vs-ride-out choice
from the SAME coin flip, so a 3-point ride-out loop was never once built. Half
the options never tried. At Bucharest the 3-point one worked where the 6-point
one didn't — and 6 was the only one it could make. My bug, introduced when I
made these more common a few builds ago.

3. The doubling-back limit was impossible to meet

It was set at 10%. Across 40 test loops, ONE passed. Real loops out of a valley
town repeat 27-68% of themselves, because the one road out is also the one road
back. A limit nothing can meet isn't strict, it's switched off: the search gave
up on it every single time, and the "this loop doubles back" note appeared on
nearly every result, which makes it meaningless.

Now 35%. It admits ordinary loops, rejects ones that repeat half of themselves,
and still rejects a plain there-and-back outright.

What you should notice

- Loops much closer to the distance you asked for.
- More variety, including more that ride out before looping.
- The "doubles back" note now appears rarely — when it does, it means it.

What to check

- Ask for 30 km and see what you actually get. Before this, results were
  routinely well off.
- Around Rasnov, look for loops that head out toward Bran or Zarnesti and loop
  there, rather than circling town.
- If a loop still repeats a lot of itself, does the note appear? It should now
  only show up on genuinely repetitive ones.

Being straight with you about one thing

Fixing the size model made the doubling-back limit the thing holding results
back, which is why I raised it. That was your call to make and you made it — but
it means loops are now allowed to repeat up to a third of themselves. If that
feels too loose in practice, the number is one line to change; tell me what
reads right on the road.

Unchanged
