Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.162 (build 165)

Both fixes from your v0.2.161 ride. Nothing else changed.

1. THE SIDE BUTTONS NO LONGER COVER THE INSTRUCTION PANEL

They now sit in the vertical middle of the screen.

What went wrong: that column was pinned at "38% down the screen, then up by
120 pixels" — a number someone picked back when the instruction panel was 58px
tall and there were five buttons. I made the panel 128px and removed one
button, and never rechecked the number. So the top button climbed onto the
panel. My mistake, and an avoidable one.

I did not just pick a new number. The column's height still changes while you
ride — the elevation button only appears on routes that have a hill profile —
so any fixed offset would drift out of date again the next time anything
changes. It now centres itself, whatever its height and whatever the panel
above it is doing.

2. THE BOTTOM BAR IS SHORTER

About 111px down to about 86px — a quarter off. All four numbers are still
there and still bigger than they were before this whole redesign:

- Time remaining 30px -> 26px (was not there at all before)
- ETA / distance / climb 17px -> 16px (they were 14px originally)
- The padding and the gap around the divider took most of the cut, not the type

Net effect across the whole screen: the HUD covered about 10% more map in
v0.2.161, now it is about 6% more than before the redesign started.

WHAT TO CHECK

- The side buttons: clear of the instruction panel now, and still easy to reach?
  If they now feel too LOW, say so — moving them is a one-line change.
- Tap the map on the right-hand edge, in the empty space above or below the
  buttons. It should still break camera-follow and go to flat overview. That
  column is now a full-height invisible strip, and I had to explicitly make it
  let taps through. If taps on the right edge do nothing, that is this.
- The bottom bar: still readable at a glance while pedalling? If the trim went
  too far, the type comes back up.
- Route-feature alerts (tunnel / unprotected left) appear bottom-right and have
  to sit above the bottom bar. The bar changed height, so their position moved
  with it. Check they do not overlap the bar or the "Steep" pill.

KNOWN, NOT A BUG

The map tap area behind the button column is invisible but live — that is
intentional, so you can still grab the map around the buttons.

UNCHANGED FROM v0.2.161

- The instruction panel itself: size, street name, the "Then" row.
- GPX import (you confirmed this works).
- The Stop button, bottom-left, same size and behaviour.
- Routing, hazards, voice, everything off this screen.
