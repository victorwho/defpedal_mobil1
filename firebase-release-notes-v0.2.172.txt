Defensive Pedal Preview v0.2.172 (build 175)

Three things in this build: the UK is now a supported country, the
"turn across traffic" warning was pointing the wrong way in left-hand-traffic
countries, and distances can now be shown in miles.

WHAT TO TEST — MILES / KILOMETRES
- Profile > Display > "Distances in miles". Flip it and walk the app: route
  preview, the peek strip, navigation HUD (distance, climb, speed), trip
  history, trip detail, community feed, City Heartbeat, loop planner, search
  results, the elevation chart's axes.
- On miles you should see: miles for distance, FEET for climbs and for short
  distances ("260 ft"), mph for speed AND for wind in the weather widget.
- Temperature stays Celsius, CO2 stays kg, weight stays kg. That is deliberate.
- Voice guidance: with voice on, an instruction should SAY "in 260 feet" —
  spelled out, not "in 260 ft". Same number you see on screen.
- Nothing should read "12.0 km" style double units or a bare number with no
  unit. If you find one, tell me the screen.

WHAT TO TEST — UK
- Fake GPS to London: Diagnostics > dev location presets > "London". Plan a
  route and confirm you get Safe/Flat/Cool/E-bike, risk colours on the map and
  a Risk Score — not the "fast routing only" fallback.
- A rider detected in the UK gets miles automatically. Because this build is an
  upgrade on your phone, you should get miles only if your app already thinks
  you are in the UK; otherwise use the Profile toggle.

WHAT TO TEST — TURN WARNING (best checked on a UK/Ireland route)
- The route-feature warning that used to say "Left turn across traffic" should
  now say "Right turn across traffic", with a mirrored arrow, when riding where
  traffic drives on the left. Everywhere else it is unchanged.

KNOWN / DELIBERATE
- Money figures are still EUR everywhere, including the UK. Separate decision.
- Distance step buttons in the loop planner read "3.1 / 6.2" on miles — those
  are the same 5 km / 10 km steps, honestly converted.

Nothing here has been on a phone before this build, so treat the whole app as
worth a look — especially anything that prints a number.
