v0.2.164 (167) — Loops now get you OUT of town

WHAT CHANGED
Loop generation had a real geometry problem: from a city centre it could not
leave the city at any distance the picker offered. Measured against the live
router, a 20 km loop from central Bucharest never got further than 1.7 km from
the start, and a 40 km one never further than 3.8 km. Bucharest's edge is about
9.8 km out.

There is now a "Where" control on the loop planner with two options:

  * Out of town (the new default) — rides out, loops out there, comes home
  * Around here — the old behaviour, a loop that rings your start

Measured improvement in reach, live router:

  Bucharest 30 km    3.9 km  ->   6.6 km
  Bucharest 60 km    8.0 km  ->  13.4 km
  Cluj      60 km    8.3 km  ->  12.6 km
  Brasov    60 km   10.4 km  ->  11.9 km

Each result card now also says how far out that loop actually gets.

WHAT TO TEST

1. Loop planner > Where. Confirm "Out of town" is selected by default and that
   tapping between the two pills re-runs the search.

2. From a city centre, generate a 30 km and a 60 km out-of-town loop. The loops
   should visibly head away from town before looping, not circle your
   neighbourhood. Check the map frames the whole loop rather than cropping it.

3. Switch to "Around here" at the same distance. Loops should stay close to the
   start, as before. This is the old behaviour and should be unchanged.

4. THE HONEST NOTE. Set "Out of town" and pick a short distance (20 or 30 km)
   while starting well inside a city. A line should appear under the pills
   naming the shortest distance that actually gets you out, e.g. "60 km is the
   shortest ride that gets you out of town from here." Then pick that distance
   and confirm the note disappears. The note should NEVER appear when you are
   already outside a town.

5. Read the "Gets N km out" line on each result and sanity-check it against the
   map.

6. Regression: saved loops still open, riding a loop still works, and
   auto-reroute is still suppressed mid-ride.

KNOWN LIMITS, DELIBERATE

* Getting out of town is expensive. Riding out and back costs about 3.5x the
  straight-line distance, so short rides genuinely cannot leave a big city.
  That is why the note exists rather than a silent city loop.

* The city boundary comes from the map provider and is an ADMINISTRATIVE
  boundary, not where the houses stop. For small communes that own surrounding
  countryside (Rasnov, Sibiu) it reads larger than the town really is.

* Out-of-town searches may come back empty more often than before, because
  every candidate now has to close a loop out where the road network is
  thinner. If that happens, tell me where and at what distance.

NOT device-tested before this build. This is the first run on a phone.
