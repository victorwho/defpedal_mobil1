Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.161 (build 164)

The navigation screen, rebuilt around the three things you reported from the bike.

Your notes were: text too small, the bottom bar repeats the top, and those
little numbers are unreadable while pedalling. All three checked out in the
code, so this build is the fix for all three.

TOP PANEL — bigger, and it now tells you what comes after

- The turn instruction went from 18px to 27px. The distance went from 16px to
  36px, with its unit ("m" / "km") on its own line underneath.
- The street name now sits under the instruction. "Turn right / Strada Traian".
- The "Then" row moved UP into the same panel. That is the fix for the
  repetition you flagged: the next turn was never a duplicate, it was the turn
  after the current one — but it was rendered at the very bottom of the screen,
  700 pixels from the turn it follows, so the two read as the same thing said
  twice. Now they sit together and read as a sequence, and you can see the next
  turn while you are still committing to the current one.

BOTTOM BAR — one number that matters, and the Stop button

- It now leads with time remaining, big. ETA, distance and climb drop to a row
  underneath, and they went from 14px to 17px.
- Speed sits next to the time remaining.
- STOP MOVED. It is now the big red block at the bottom-left of that bar,
  roughly 8x the area it had. The small red circle on the right-hand button
  column is GONE — do not go looking for it. Everything else in that column
  (recenter, voice, hazard, elevation) is exactly where it was.
- Stop still asks before it does anything: Keep riding / Discard / Save. One
  tap to open it, same as before.

WHAT TO CHECK

- Is 27px actually readable at your handlebar distance, in daylight? That is
  the whole point of this build. If it is still too small, say so and I will
  push it further — it is a one-line change.
- The new Stop button is bigger and sits right where your thumb rests. Tell me
  if you hit it by accident. It is confirm-gated so a stray tap costs you a
  dismissal, not a ride, but if it happens often that is a design problem.
- IMPORTED GPX COURSE. Courses from Komoot/Strava carry no street names, so the
  street line should simply not be there — no blank gap under the instruction.
  If you see an empty line, that is a bug.
- A ROUTE WITH A TUNNEL OR AN UNPROTECTED LEFT TURN. Those alerts pop up
  bottom-right and have to sit ABOVE the bottom bar. The bar got taller, so I
  moved them up by a calculated amount — calculated, not measured on a real
  phone. If an alert overlaps the "Steep" pill or the bottom bar, that is this.
- A MULTI-STOP ROUTE. The "Stop 2 of 3" header and the skip control should look
  normal, and the red Stop block should stay the same size as on a normal ride
  rather than stretching into a tall slab.
- Romanian. The longest turn phrases at the new size — anything clipped?

THE TRADE, STATED PLAINLY

The HUD now covers about 10% more of the map than it did — roughly 150px of
screen became roughly 240px, top and bottom combined. That is the cost of
legibility and I made the call deliberately. If it feels like too much of the
map is gone, tell me and I will trim the type back a couple of steps.

UNCHANGED

- Routing, the map, voice guidance, hazard reporting.
- The right-hand button column, other than losing the stop button.
- What happens when you end a ride: same dialog, same save/discard, same
  "why did you end early" question.
- Android back button still ends a ride.
