Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.142 (build 145)

Two things: loops can now ride OUT to somewhere better and loop there, and the
download is 40% smaller.

1. Ride out, loop there, ride home

Some places have the good riding somewhere other than where you live. A town
beside mountains gets a better ride by heading to the foothills and looping in
the hills than by circling its own suburbs.

Every third candidate now centres its loop away from the start, spending about
a third of the distance on getting there and back. There is no new setting —
the Terrain preference already steers toward these where it matters, because a
loop in the foothills measures hillier than one round the town. Ask for Hilly
near mountains and you should see the difference.

The doubling-back cap had to learn about this. A route that rides out and back
to reach its loop repeats that approach by definition, and the 10% cap would
have rejected the exact shape you asked for. So the cap now measures doubling
back INSIDE the loop only, and ignores a deliberate approach. Backtracking
within the loop is still capped exactly as before, and a pure out-and-back with
no loop at the end is still rejected outright — that is not a loop.

Honest note on what this does and does not do: it puts the loop somewhere
else, which is the shape asked for. It does not make loops easier to find. In
testing around Brasov, these pass the doubling-back cap about as often as
ordinary loops do, not more. And in practice the router usually finds a
different way home rather than retracing the approach, so the "stem" is often
not a stem at all — you just get a loop that starts and ends further out.

2. The download is much smaller

214 MB down to 127 MB. The old build carried native code for processor
architectures no phone in this test group can run — x86 and x86_64 exist for
emulators and were 83 MB of dead weight on every install. A tester install
failed for lack of space because of it.

What to try
- Somewhere with hills or mountains nearby: ask for Hilly at 25-40 km and see
  whether any result heads out of town before looping.
- Compare against Flat at the same distance — you should get more local rings.
- If a result rides out and back on the same road for a long stretch, that is
  worth reporting; the approach is meant to be a means to an end, not the ride.

Unchanged
- "Start ride" confirms in place; it does not launch navigation yet.
- You cannot save a loop.
- Off-route on a loop shows the off-course banner and will not reroute you.

Only works in the 31 countries our safety road data covers.
