Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.153 (build 156)

The unpaved numbers just got a lot bigger, and that is correct.

The routing server changed yesterday (9 Sept). It now recognises forest paths
and bridleways that carry no surface information in the map data as unpaved.
Before, those were invisible to it — so "avoid unpaved" could not avoid them and
the percentages did not count them.

Nothing about your routes changed. The same roads are the same roads. What
changed is that the app can now see what they are.

Concretely, on Brasov -> Rasnov:

  before      15.7% unpaved
  now         63.1% unpaved

That is the same ride, described honestly for the first time. Expect the
percentages you see around Rasnov to roughly quadruple.

"Avoid unpaved" is now genuinely strong

On that same route, asking for paved gets you a fully paved line for an extra
377 metres. Detours will often be that cheap.

But in remote areas there may be NO paved way through at all. Previously the app
handled that badly: it fell back to a plain fast route, so if you asked for a
SAFE and PAVED route you could silently get one that was neither — no safety
routing and no surface filter, with nothing said.

Now: it asks again without the paved constraint and TELLS you. On loops you will
see "No paved loop exists here — this one uses unpaved sections" on the result.
You will never again be handed something quietly different from what you asked
for.

What to check

- Look at the surface percentages around Rasnov. They should be much higher than
  last build. Do they now match what you actually ride?
- Turn on "avoid unpaved" and plan something into the mountains. Either you get a
  paved route, or you get a clear note that none exists. Both are fine; silence
  is not.
- If you see the "no paved loop exists" note somewhere you think a paved loop
  clearly does exist, tell me.

One judgement call I want to flag

The server team reported the new figures using a coarser way of measuring than
the app uses: theirs counts a whole section of road as unpaved if any part of it
is, where the app splits the section proportionally. On one test route that is
the difference between 47.8% and 25.7% — theirs counts 6.3 km of a partly-paved
stretch entirely as unpaved.

I kept the app's finer measurement, so numbers you see here will read slightly
LOWER than any figures quoted from the routing side. That is deliberate, and the
app's is the more accurate of the two. If the percentages feel understated
against what you ride, that is worth knowing and I will look again.

Unchanged
- Five loops per search, detour detection, saved loops syncing to your account.
- Scenic scoring still covers the Rasnov area only.
