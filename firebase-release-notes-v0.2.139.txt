Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.139 (build 142)

NEW: loops now prefer to come back a different way

Riding up a hill and back down the same road is not much of a loop. The
generator now measures how much of each candidate is ridden twice and prefers
the one that doubles back least.

The measurement is exact rather than a guess. Every route comes back with the
list of map nodes it passes through, so a stretch counts as doubled back when
the same pair of nodes is traversed twice — in either direction, since going
up a road and coming down it is exactly the case worth catching. Both passes
count: an out-and-back spur contributes its whole length twice, which is what
you would say if asked.

This deliberately does not use "how close are these two lines on the map". A
canal towpath running beside a road, or the two halves of a dual carriageway,
would look like doubling back to that test when they are the opposite — a
genuine different way home.

There is no new setting. Nobody sets out wanting to ride the same hill twice,
so this is always on rather than a fifth control to configure.

Where it sits: below safety, above everything else. A loop that doubles back
but stays off dangerous roads still beats a clean loop that does not. Above
distance accuracy and above the offroad preference though — a 16 km loop that
comes back a different way beats a 15 km one that retraces, because coming
back a different way is what makes it a loop at all.

Small overlaps are ignored. Loops routinely share a few metres through the
junction you started from, and that is not doubling back.

When a loop does retrace meaningfully, the result says so — "Doubles back on
34% of itself" under the distance. Clean loops say nothing, which should be
most of them.

What to try
- Somewhere with plenty of roads, results should mostly show no note at all.
- Try a dead-end valley or a peninsula, where a clean loop may be impossible.
  You should still get a loop, honestly labelled, rather than nothing.
- If you see a note on a loop that looks clean on the map, that is worth
  reporting — it would mean the node measurement is disagreeing with reality.

Unchanged from build 141
- "Start ride" confirms in place; it does not launch navigation yet.
- You cannot save a loop.
- Off-route on a loop shows the off-course banner and will not reroute you.

Only works in the 31 countries our safety road data covers.
