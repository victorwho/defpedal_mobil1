Defensive Pedal — Preview v0.2.149 (build 152)

CHANGED: how loops are shown.

1. The map is now the result

Tap Find loops and the panel drops out of the way. The map re-centres on you,
zoomed out far enough to see a loop of the size you asked for, and the loops
draw on one at a time as each is found. The strip at the bottom keeps you posted
("Finding loops… 3 of 10").

The loops were always drawing in like that — it just happened behind a panel
covering most of the screen, so nobody ever saw it.

When the results are in, the panel rises to a NEW middle height: map on top,
list below, both usable. Drag it up for the controls, down to get the map back.
Tapping the handle now cycles through all three heights.

2. The numbers on each loop are readable now

Distance, time and climb were already on every result — squeezed into one small
grey line, separated by dots, all the same size. Three different kinds of number
looking identical reads as decoration, so the eye skips it. They are now three
labelled figures, aligned so you can compare down the column.

3. The time estimate is YOURS

Every rider was being shown distance ÷ 15 km/h. Always. The code had a note
saying it would use your riding history, but nothing ever did.

It now uses the middle speed of your actual rides. Take a long coffee stop on
one ride and it will barely move — that is why it uses the middle value rather
than an average. Hills are counted too, so a climb-heavy loop is not quoted at
flat-ground speed.

Until you have three rides logged it still says 15 km/h — but now it SAYS so:
"Est. time at 15 km/h". You should see this switch to your own pace once you
have a few rides in.

4. Tap a loop for surface and roads

Tapping a result opens it in place (tap again to close) and shows:

- Surface — a bar plus "78% paved / 22% unpaved". Always shown, including at
  100% paved. Before, a fully-paved loop showed nothing at all, which looked
  identical to "we didn't measure it".
- Roads — how much of the loop is on busy roads, which is the thing that should
  decide between two otherwise-similar loops.

It says "unpaved", not "gravel", on purpose: the map data tells us a road is not
paved, never what it is instead. Could be gravel, dirt, cobbles or a forest
track.

What to check

- Run a search and watch the map — do the loops drawing in actually read as
  something happening, or does it feel like the app went blank?
- The middle height of the panel: right, too tall, too short?
- Does the panel moving on its own feel helpful, or like the app grabbing the
  wheel? This is the one I would most like your opinion on. If it is the latter,
  I can make the auto-drop optional without unwinding the rest.
- Your time estimate — does it look like your real pace? If you have fewer than
  three rides logged it will still say "at 15 km/h".
- Tap each of the five loops and check the surface split looks plausible for
  where they go.

Not included

- Tapping a route line on the MAP still does nothing; selection is in the list,
  as agreed. Two overlapping loops would have had no way to say which you meant.

Unchanged
- Five results per search, all measured.
- Saved loops sync to your account.
- Scenic scoring still covers the Rasnov area only.
